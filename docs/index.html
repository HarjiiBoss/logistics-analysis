Analysis

What the Data Reveals

Delivery Reliability
Fewer Than Half of Deliveries Arrive On Time
Only 44.6% of deliveries met their scheduled time, against 66.7% of pickups. The 55.7% reported earlier combines the two; deliveries run late far more often than pickups.
01
Driver Performance
No Driver Stands Out From the Rest
Driver on-time delivery rates range from 37.6% to 50.0% across 124 drivers with about 650 deliveries each. A chi-square test gives p = 0.413: the spread is what chance alone would produce, so the data cannot single out better or worse drivers.
02
Route Profitability
Every Route Keeps 59–78% of Revenue After Fuel
With fuel estimated from gallons used on each trip, margin after fuel runs from 59.2% (RTE00037) to 77.7% (RTE00015), median 72.0%. No route loses money on fuel; driver pay, maintenance and other costs are not in the data at route level.
03
Data Quality
Fuel Purchase Records Cannot Cost Individual Trips
Fuel purchases are linked to trips, but their cost per trip has no relationship with trip distance (correlation 0.00), while gallons used tracks distance closely (0.98). Using the purchase link had produced a false –609% margin on a 94-mile route; the analysis now uses gallons used.
04
Fleet Health
Maintenance Spend Is Spread Across the Fleet
Maintenance totals $5.7M across 120 trucks. The costliest 10% of trucks account for 15.2% of spend, close to an even share. TRK00040, TRK00026 and TRK00014 have the highest cost per mile at about $0.06, against a median of $0.037.
05
Data Scope
14 Relational Tables · 85,000+ Trip Records Processed
The pipeline covered trips, loads, drivers, routes, trucks, delivery events, fuel purchases and maintenance records. 80,458 trips remained after cleaning; every driver's fuel efficiency falls between 6.45 and 6.57 MPG.
06
Analytical Flag

Fuel purchase costs do not match the trips they are linked to, so the fuel join was checked against gallons used, an independent measure, before route margins were reported. The other figures come from single tables or joins on trip and load IDs.

Business Insights

Structured Analytical Insights

01
Delivery Reliability
Investigate Late Deliveries by Route, Facility and Time Window
Pickups are on time two-thirds of the time; deliveries less than half. Because drivers do not differ by more than chance, the next step is to compare late and on-time deliveries by route, distance, facility and scheduled window.
02
Route Strategy
Rank Routes on Full Cost Before Changing Prices
Margin after fuel varies from 59% to 78%, so no route is losing money on fuel alone. A pricing review needs driver pay, tolls and maintenance by route; until then the 18-point spread is the only route-level signal.
03
Maintenance
Review the Highest Cost-per-Mile Trucks Individually
Spend is not concentrated in a few vehicles, so fleet-wide changes would affect most trucks. TRK00040, TRK00026 and TRK00014 run at about $0.06 per mile against a $0.037 median and are the first candidates for a replacement-versus-repair review.
04
Data Governance
Fix the Link Between Fuel Purchases and Trips
Fuel card purchases cannot currently be traced to the trips they fuelled. Recording the trip or truck correctly at purchase would allow actual fuel cost per route and per driver, in place of the estimate used here.
Methodology

3-Notebook Analytical Pipeline

Notebook 01
Data Exploration
Loaded all 14 tables from raw CSV files. Checked null distribution, verified foreign key relationships across tables, assessed data types and row counts.
Notebook 02
Data Cleaning
Fixed null values, converted data types, standardised categorical fields, and exported clean CSVs for analysis. All cleaning decisions documented inline.
Notebook 03
Analysis
Computed on-time delivery rates (deliveries only) and tested whether drivers differ, estimated route margin after fuel from gallons used, and calculated maintenance cost per mile.
Tableau
Dashboard Build
Packaged analysis outputs into an interactive Tableau Public dashboard with driver ranking, route profitability, and fleet maintenance views.
